# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-07)

First release.

- `init`, `generate`, `status` and `paid` commands
- Reads Toggl Track, Clockify, Harvest and generic CSV exports
- Groups line items by project, description, day or entry; rounding is configurable
- NET payment terms, optional tax line, and an optional time-log page
- CSV ledger that numbers invoices and blocks duplicate months
